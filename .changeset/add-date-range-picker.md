Add `DateRangePicker` — `DatePicker`'s two-ended sibling for picking a custom start/end window, complementing `TimeRange`'s fixed presets ("1h", "24h") for whenever a reader needs an arbitrary range. `Calendar` now also supports `mode="range"`, styling the days between the two picked ends with a softer fill distinct from the two endpoints.
