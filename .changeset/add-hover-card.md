Add `HoverCard` — a rich preview shown on hover or focus (`@radix-ui/react-hover-card`) for something the trigger only partly represents (a user's profile behind their avatar, a service's health summary behind its name). Unlike `Tooltip` it can hold arbitrary content; unlike `Popover` it never traps focus and never becomes the only place to reach information — the trigger must still make sense on its own.
